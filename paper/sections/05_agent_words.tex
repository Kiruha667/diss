Detected success claims are as frequent for unresolved as for resolved runs: \numsrc{g0.ne.claim.fail@L229}{$37.1\%$} versus \numsrc{g0.ne.claim.ok@L228}{$35.6\%$} on the article population; within a model, resolved runs carry slightly more of them (Table~\ref{tab:agent-words}). The detector misses claims, and the corrected estimates come from an audit of \numsrc{g0.hc.n@L216}{$100$} messages from the earlier population of \numsrc{e15.all.runs@L99}{$7\,864$} submitted runs including empty patches, with model-assisted labels reviewed by the author. That audit detects only differences larger than \numsrc{rev.g0.power@L249}{$24\,\mathrm{pp}$}, so the corrected association remains unestablished.

Adding ``Be honest'' to the question does not improve discrimination on finished attempts; that comparison uses \numsrc{e11b.pairs@L38}{$112$} pairs, and C4 was not repeated in the confirmatory check. The plain question gives a weak signal across all \numsrc{e16.pairs.all@L161}{$1\,475$} finished-attempt pairs, including retained answers,\footnote{The structure analysis defines finishing by a last agent message without a tool call; elicitation requires a non-empty final message, yielding \numsrc{e16.pairs.all@L161}{$1\,475$} rather than \numsrc{e15k.dev.pairs.ff@L115}{$1\,493$} devstral-2512 pairs.} but is not confirmed on the \numsrc{e16.pairs.new@L164}{$595$} pairs containing only new answers and is no stronger than the trajectory model with the agent's own check outcomes ($S+O$). Answers concentrate at \numsrc{e16.mostly@L156}{$95\,\mathrm{or}\,100$} percent for both outcomes.
